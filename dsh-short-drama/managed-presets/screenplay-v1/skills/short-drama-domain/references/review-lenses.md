# Optional review lenses

Signal-channel-B review is advisory. Select only the lenses useful for the
task and present disagreements instead of collapsing them into one rule:

- **Story**: objective, pressure, reversal, consequence, and character choice.
- **Direction**: rhythm, visual action, playable conflict, and scene economy.
- **Audience**: information gaps, payoff timing, retention, and commercial
  clarity.
- **Dialogue**: knowledge boundaries, subtext, voice distinction, and
  performability.

The lenses may identify risks or propose alternatives. They never block a
commit and never replace the user's creative decision.
